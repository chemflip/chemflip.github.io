ChemFlip Privacy Policy

Applies to ChemFlip: General Chemistry and ChemFlip: Organic Chemistry on iPhone, iPad, Android, Windows and the web. Effective November 16, 2026.

ChemFlip is a chemistry practice app for college and university students, made by Dr. Vahid Rezaei. This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have. In short: your practice data stays on your device, there are no ads, no tracking, and we never sell your information.

Information kept on your device

  • Your profile: name, school, course, and optional details such as class section, instructor, instructor email and student ID.
  • Your profile photo, if you take or upload one.
  • Your answers, scores, mistakes, bookmarks, streaks and settings.

This information is stored only on your device unless you use the class features described below.

Class features (optional)

If you join or create a class, the app sends the following to our class service so that you, your classmates and your instructor can see it:

  • Your display name, avatar or a small version of your profile photo, and school.
  • Your student ID, only if you entered one (so your instructor can match test scores to the gradebook).
  • Your practice totals (questions answered, correct answers, time per question), progress by chapter, weak topics, and class or team test results.
  • Messages and results you choose to post in the class feed or a study group.

School comparisons

To compare schools, the app sends your school name, school type (community college or university), course, and practice totals (questions answered, correct answers, time) with a random ID, not your name. Only averages for schools with at least 3 students are shown. You can turn this off in Settings, which also deletes these totals.

Your instructor can see the progress of students in their class. Classmates can see names, avatars, leaderboard positions, test rankings and posts. The class service is hosted by Supabase in the United States.

Reviews and reports

If you send a review or report a post, we receive what you write, your star rating, your name and school as entered in the app, and the course you selected. Reviews are read only by the developer; the app shows just the average rating.

Camera and photos

The camera and photo library are used only when you choose to add a profile photo. The app never accesses them in the background.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple or Google. We do not receive your payment details.

What we do not do

  • No advertising and no ad tracking.
  • No selling or renting of personal information.
  • No third-party analytics.
  • No collection of precise location, contacts or health data.

Children

ChemFlip is designed for college and university students and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your information

You can delete everything at any time in the app: Settings → Delete my data. This removes your profile and progress from your device and deletes your class memberships, scores, test results and posts from the class service. Instructors can remove students and posts from their classes. You can also email us to request deletion.

Security

Information sent to the class service travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

Email addresses

News emails (optional). If you enter your email and tick the box, ChemFlip stores your email, name, school, role and course so we can send you news and updates about ChemFlip. You can unsubscribe at any time by replying "unsubscribe" or writing to chemflipp@gmail.com. We never sell or share your email.

Instructor verification. To use instructor tools, instructors enter an academic email ending in .edu. We send a one-time 6-digit code to that address through our database provider (Supabase) to confirm it. The address is used only for this verification.

Invites. When you use a friend's invite code, we record an anonymous app identifier with the code so the person who invited you can receive their discount. No names or emails are attached to invites.
